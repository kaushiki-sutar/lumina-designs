About Lumina Designs

Welcome to The Maison—the editorial heart of Lumina Designs, where timeless fashion meets modern elegance. Our curated collections are crafted with precision and passion, reflecting our commitment to quality and style. Explore our range of exquisite designs that celebrate individuality and sophistication.

From refined essentials to statement pieces, Lumina Designs presents fashion for women, men, and children with a focus on quality, comfort, and modern luxury. Every detail reflects a commitment to creating an elevated shopping experience through clean aesthetics and carefully selected collections.

Design Inspiration

The visual identity of Lumina Designs was inspired by the timeless elegance of leading fashion houses and editorial fashion photography. Clean layouts, sophisticated typography, and minimalist design principles were carefully chosen to create a refined and immersive browsing experience.

Inspiration for this project was drawn from renowned brands including Ralph Lauren and Dior, along with the artistic direction of Vogue. Their distinctive approach to fashion presentation influenced the overall aesthetic, while Lumina Designs was developed as an original educational project with its own layout and identity.
